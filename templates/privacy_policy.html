Panchtatva Worldwide Business Private Limited ("PWBL", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data we collect through our website and services.

We may collect, use, and process the following categories of data:

  • Contact Information: Name, business email address, phone number, company name, and geographical market.

Your submitted data is utilized strictly for professional business purposes, including:
